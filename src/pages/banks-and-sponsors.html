How we work

Partner-led by design, not by necessity.

Our products are built to run on licensed partners’ permissions and rails from day one. That is a deliberate architecture choice: it puts regulated activity where regulation already sits, and makes the division of responsibility legible to both sides.

Clear perimeter

We are explicit, in writing, about which activities sit with the partner institution and which sit with us. Ambiguity in that boundary is what makes these relationships fail later.

Regulator-aligned sequencing

The product roadmap is developed in step with the Central Bank of Nigeria’s framework and launched with licensed partners rather than ahead of them.

KYC and source of funds

Counterparty onboarding requires identification on both sides and source-of-funds evidence before value moves. This is a gate, not a preference.

Documented commercial terms

Our commercial relationships are papered — scope, exclusivity, non-circumvention and fee terms — before they operate, not after a dispute.
