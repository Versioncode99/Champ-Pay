What changes

Three problems, addressed directly.

No terminal to manage

Acceptance runs on the Android phone your staff already carry. Adding a till point at a busy hour becomes handing someone a phone, not sourcing a device.

Money that arrives today

Instant and same-day settlement is designed in, not sold as a premium tier. Your cash cycle should not be financing somebody else’s float.

Books that already match

Takings by day, by site and by staff member, reconciled against what actually landed — so the month-end is a review rather than an investigation.

Straight answers

The questions merchants actually ask.

Can I use this today?

Not yet. Merchant acceptance is in build, and the pilot opens to a limited number of businesses first. Registering your interest puts you in that group and costs you nothing.

Do I need to buy anything?

The point of the product is that you do not. An NFC-capable Android phone is the terminal.

What does it cost?

Pricing is set before the pilot opens and will be published rather than negotiated case by case for small merchants. We are not going to quote a number here that we might have to change.

Who actually holds the money?

Settlement runs through licensed partner institutions. That relationship is set out properly with every merchant before they take a single payment.

The morning after

Yesterday, reconciled before you open.

The part of acceptance nobody demos is the bit that costs you a Sunday. Takings split by method, fees shown as a line rather than buried in a net figure, and a settlement time you can plan a stock order around.

If your current provider makes you export a spreadsheet to find out what you actually earned on Friday, that is the problem we are building against.

Being straight

What we can and cannot do for you today.

You are running a business, not evaluating a roadmap. Here is the honest split so you can decide whether a conversation is worth your time right now.

Worth talking now
  • You want a place in the first merchant group and will give feedback that shapes the product.
  • Terminal cost, breakage or charging is a real operational problem for you.
  • Slow settlement is affecting how you buy stock or pay staff.
  • You run several sites and reconciliation across them is manual.
Come back to us later
  • You need to switch provider this month — we are not there yet, and we will not pretend otherwise.
  • You need card acceptance live on day one under our own licence.
  • You are looking for the cheapest rate on the market. We do not intend to compete on price.
  • You want consumer or peer-to-peer features. Every line we run is business-facing.
