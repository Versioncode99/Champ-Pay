The line

Wholesale, not retail.

We source digital-asset liquidity at institutional tiers and make it available to counterparties who need size, consistency and a settlement path they can reconcile — rather than a retail order book and a screenshot.

Who it serves

Licensed exchanges, payment companies and treasury desks in African markets who are moving genuine volume and being priced as though they are not.

What it provides

Consistent institutional pricing, defined settlement windows, and a counterparty relationship with terms written down rather than agreed in a chat window.

How it is governed

KYC on both sides, source-of-funds checks, and written agreements before anything moves. There is no version of this business that works without that.

On detail: commercial terms, routing, counterparties and the structure behind this line are shared under NDA with qualifying counterparties, not published on a website. If you are an exchange, fintech or desk with real volume, the conversation starts at contact.

Do you publish spreads or rates?

No. Pricing is quoted to a counterparty against their size and settlement requirements, under agreement. Published numbers on a marketing page would be misleading for both of us.

Do you serve retail?

No. This line is business to business only.

What is required before onboarding?

KYC on both sides, source-of-funds evidence, and a written agreement. We do not begin with a transfer and paperwork afterwards.

Is the price table on this page tradable?

No. It is third-party public reference data displayed for market context and nothing else.
