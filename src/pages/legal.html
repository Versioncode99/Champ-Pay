Legal & privacy

What we do with your information.

Short, and in plain language, because a privacy notice nobody can read is not a privacy notice.

Who we are

This website is operated by ChampPay. The business is being incorporated in Nigeria as Champ Pay Nigeria Limited; until that registration completes, “we” and “us” in this notice mean the ChampPay business and the people running it.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us. If you complete the contact form, we receive your name, email address, company, the capacity you selected, and your message. There is no account to create, no profile built about you, and no tracking pixel following you elsewhere.

Why we hold it

To reply to you, and to continue the conversation you started. That is the whole purpose. We do not sell your details, rent them, or pass them to anyone marketing something to you.

How long we keep it

For as long as the enquiry is live, and afterwards only where there is a genuine business or legal reason to retain it. Ask us to delete your details and we will, unless we are required to keep a record.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to us through the contact page and say which. We will respond within a reasonable period and without making it difficult.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no advertising cookies and runs no third-party analytics or tracking scripts. Two ordinary technical points are worth stating plainly:

  • Fonts. Typefaces are served by Google Fonts, so loading a page makes a request to Google’s servers, which will see your IP address as part of serving that request.
  • Hosting. The site is served through Cloudflare, which processes requests and keeps standard technical logs for security and performance.
  • Market and reference data. Several pages show public market data. Your browser fetches it directly from the source, not from us, and no request carries anything about you beyond what any web request carries. The sources are: Binance (a live price stream on the digital assets page), CoinGecko (market capitalisation and 7-day change), Coinbase (used only as a fallback if the live stream cannot be reached), open.er-api.com (currency reference rates) and the World Bank Open Data API (published Nigeria indicators). None of these requires an account or a key, and we receive nothing back from them about you.

Security

Messages sent through this site travel over HTTPS. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect what you send us. No transmission over the internet is ever entirely without risk, and we would rather say that than imply otherwise.

Using this website

The content here is provided for general information about ChampPay and the markets we work in. It may change without notice as the business develops.

  • Market figures. Where we cite market data, it is published by third parties and attributed as reported. It describes the market, not ChampPay’s own performance.
  • Forward-looking statements. Descriptions of what we are building, and any projections, reflect current plans and intentions. Plans change.
  • Not an offer. Nothing on this website is an offer of securities, an invitation to invest, or advice of any kind. Any commercial or investment discussion happens under separate documentation, with appropriate professional advice on both sides.

Links out

Where we link to another organisation’s site, we are not responsible for its content or its privacy practices. Their notice applies once you are there.

Changes to this notice

We update this page when what we do changes. The version you are reading is the current one.

Contact

Questions about this notice, or about anything we hold on you, go through the contact page. Mark the message “privacy” and it will be routed accordingly.
