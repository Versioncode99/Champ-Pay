The line

Wholesale, not retail.

The ChampPay model is role-separated: qualify the institutional need, establish authority and controls, then document pricing and settlement privately rather than treating a retail order book as an institutional process.

Who it serves

Licensed exchanges, payment companies and treasury desks in African markets who are moving genuine volume and being priced as though they are not.

What it provides

Consistent institutional pricing, defined settlement windows, and a counterparty relationship with terms written down rather than agreed in a chat window.

How it is governed

KYC on both sides, source-of-funds checks, and written agreements before anything moves. There is no version of this business that works without that.

On detail: commercial terms, routing, counterparties and transaction structure are shared only through an authorised private process. If you are an exchange, fintech or treasury desk, write to contact@champ-pay.com.

Qualification

Institutional starts with controls.

Public price is context. A real conversation begins with the parties, purpose, authority, source of funds and settlement requirements.

01

Identify

Confirm the organisation, authorised people and institutional purpose.

02

Qualify

Establish jurisdiction, source of funds, asset, direction and expected size.

03

Protect

Execute appropriate confidentiality and onboarding documentation.

04

Document

Set out executable price, settlement, responsibilities and exception handling in writing.

Do you publish spreads or rates?

No. Pricing is quoted to a counterparty against their size and settlement requirements, under agreement. Published numbers on a marketing page would be misleading for both of us.

Do you serve retail?

No. This line is business to business only.

What is required before onboarding?

KYC on both sides, source-of-funds evidence, and a written agreement. We do not begin with a transfer and paperwork afterwards.

Is the price table on this page tradable?

No. It is third-party public reference data displayed for market context and nothing else.
