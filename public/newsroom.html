For press

What we will and will not say on the record.

Stated up front so a deadline is not spent discovering it.

On the record
  • The market analysis on this site, and the third-party sources behind it.
  • What we are building, at what stage, and what is not built yet.
  • Our read on Nigerian acceptance economics and the terminal question.
  • Corrections. If we get something wrong here, we will say so on this page.
Not for publication
  • Counterparty and partner names before an agreement is executed.
  • Commercial terms, pricing, spreads or routing.
  • Funding specifics, valuation or investor identities.
  • Anything about a merchant's own volumes. That is their information, not ours.

Attribution. Market figures we publish are third-party data, cited as reported, and describe Nigeria rather than ChampPay. If you are quoting a number from this site, quote the original publisher — and tell us if you think we have read it wrong.
