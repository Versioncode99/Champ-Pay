Merchant acceptance

The terminal can be the phone already in the merchant’s hand.

SoftPOS turns a compatible Android handset into a contactless acceptance surface. QR and proximity methods widen the entry points; one operational layer keeps the records together.

The interface is only the visible edge. Routing, risk, reconciliation and settlement design decide whether the experience holds together after the tap.

The market

African commerce is already digital. Merchant infrastructure has to catch up.

The interesting question is no longer whether people will pay electronically. It is whether the business taking the payment gets a resilient, comprehensible and affordable operating experience.

That is why the phone, the settlement trail and the merchant record belong in the same conversation.
