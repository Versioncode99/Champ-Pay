About us

Built in Lagos, for the way Lagos actually trades.

ChampPay is a multi-service, multi-currency payments company. We are building acceptance, settlement and treasury infrastructure for African businesses — and we are building it from inside the market rather than describing it from outside.

Why we exist

The infrastructure question is not solved just because the volume arrived.

Nigeria’s move to electronic payments is one of the fastest anywhere. That story gets told as a triumph, and largely it is one. But underneath the headline volume sits a merchant who still has to buy a terminal, still waits days for settlement, and still reconciles by hand on a Sunday night.

We think the next decade of African payments belongs to whoever serves that merchant properly — not with another consumer wallet, but with the layer underneath: acceptance that needs no hardware, settlement that arrives while they are trading, and eventually the ability to hold and move the currencies their business actually runs on.
