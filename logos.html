Seven directions to choose from

Each is a different idea, not a variation of the same one. Every mark is shown large, at 32px and 20px, and reversed on a light ground, because a mark that only works big is not finished. Refer to them by their reference code.

01

Floodlight

The current mark. Lamp cells in a floodlight head, the lit ones describing a C. Ties directly to the site's idea, but reads abstract and a little technical at a glance.

02

The Bowl

A single heavy C with flat-cut terminals, drawn like a stadium bowl in plan. The most institutional option: quiet, confident, embosses and engraves cleanly, and will not date.

03

Rank

Three ascending chevrons: championship rank insignia, and a fast-forward. Reads as speed and seniority at once, which is the pitch. Strongest of the set at very small sizes.

04

Laurel

A championship wreath reduced to two arcs, opening at the top like a crown. Warmer than the rest of the set. The weakest at 20px, where the two strokes start to close up.

05

The Plate

A championship plate holding the C. Heraldic and solid, the shape banks and card schemes have used for a century. Carries the most authority on a letterhead or a contract.

06

Strip Weave

A C built from woven strips, after Aso-oke and kente narrow-strip weaving. African without a map or a sunset, and a strip is literally a rail. The most ownable of the seven.

07

Podium

A winner's podium that is also a rising bar chart. The most literal read of "Pay Like a Champ" and the most immediately legible to a non-designer. Risk: it can read generic-fintech.
