Before you write

Things worth knowing, so you can skip a round trip.

Most first messages ask one of these. Here are the answers, so your message can be about the interesting part instead.

Are you live? Can I take payments today?

Not yet. The merchant acceptance product is in build and the first merchant group has not opened. If you need to switch provider this month we are the wrong call, and we would rather say that now than take a meeting on it.

What licences do you hold?

None directly. Regulated activity runs on licensed partners' permissions while our own authorisations are worked. The detail, including what is not held, is set out for risk teams on the banks and sponsors page.

Will you sign an NDA before we talk?

Yes, and for introducers an NCNDA before any name is exchanged. That is the normal order and we do not ask anyone to invert it.

Do you publish pricing?

No. Pricing depends on volume, method mix and settlement terms, and a headline rate on a marketing page would be misleading for both of us. Ask and you will get a real number against your real volume.

Where are you based?

The business is being built for the Nigerian market with the relationships held directly in-market. Correspondence goes through this form so it lands with the right person rather than an inbox nobody owns.

I am a supplier or agency. Should I write?

Honestly, probably not right now — we are small and building, and unsolicited services pitches will get a polite no. If you genuinely hold a piece of the payments stack, the partners page is the better door.

On response times. We say two business days and we mean it, including when the answer is no. If you have not heard back inside that window, the message did not reach us — send it again rather than assuming you were ignored.
