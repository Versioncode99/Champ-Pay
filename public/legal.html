Legal, privacy & confidentiality

Clear boundaries for public and private information.

This notice explains the website, the enquiry data ChampPay receives and how a sensitive business conversation should begin.

PrivacyConfidentialityWebsite terms

Privacy notice

Who is responsible

ChampPay is responsible for the personal data submitted through this website. Privacy questions and rights requests can be sent to contact@champ-pay.com with “privacy” in the subject line.

What we collect

The contact form asks for your name, work email, company or organisation, your role and the message you choose to send. We also receive ordinary security and delivery information generated when a web request is made, such as an IP address, timestamp and browser information in infrastructure logs.

Why we use it

We use enquiry data to assess your request, respond, route it to the appropriate person, maintain a record of the conversation and protect the website against misuse. The applicable basis is taking steps at your request and our legitimate interest in handling genuine business enquiries, securing the site and keeping proportionate records.

Who may receive it

Access is limited to people handling the enquiry and service providers needed to host the site and deliver the message. Cloudflare serves and protects the website; the configured email-delivery provider sends the form message; the receiving email service stores it. Professional advisers may receive relevant information where necessary and subject to appropriate duties.

International processing

Web hosting and email providers may process data outside Nigeria. Where an international transfer occurs, we use providers and contractual or organisational safeguards appropriate to the service and the information involved.

How long we keep it

A routine enquiry is normally retained for up to 12 months after the last substantive contact. We may keep a narrower record for longer where a commercial relationship, dispute, security event or legal obligation makes that necessary. Data that is no longer needed should be deleted or de-identified.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or a portable copy, and may withdraw consent where consent is the basis used. We may need to verify your identity before acting. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

The site uses HTTPS and reasonable technical and organisational controls. Do not send passwords, keys, card information, account credentials, transaction files, identity documents or confidential attachments through the first-contact form.

Cookies, fonts and reference data

This site does not run advertising pixels or behavioural analytics. Google Fonts receives the ordinary network information required to serve the typefaces. Cloudflare processes web requests and technical logs for delivery and security.

Public reference data may be fetched from open.er-api.com, the World Bank, Binance, CoinGecko or Coinbase. Those services receive an ordinary web request when their data is loaded; their own privacy notices apply. Rates and market figures are contextual and are not transaction quotes.

Confidentiality

Submitting a form does not create an NDA.

The public website intentionally omits non-public corporate, regulatory, commercial, partner, customer, financing, roadmap and diligence information. A contact-form submission, email or introductory call does not by itself create confidentiality, exclusivity, agency or a commercial relationship.

If useful next steps require sensitive information, ask for an appropriate written NDA, NCNDA or diligence protocol and wait until it has been executed by authorised parties. Until then, describe organisations by role rather than name and keep private terms, personal data and documents out of the message.

Public names and marks

ChampPay does not publish a partner, customer, counterparty, investor or referral relationship merely because a conversation exists. Any public use of another organisation’s name or mark requires appropriate authority.

Website terms

General information

The site explains market problems, product concepts and the kinds of private conversations ChampPay supports. It is general information, not an executable quote, legal or financial advice, a commitment to provide a service, or a substitute for written commercial documents.

Third-party figures and links

Market figures are attributed to third-party publishers and describe the relevant market, not ChampPay performance. We do not control external sites and are not responsible for their content, availability or privacy practices.

Intellectual property

The ChampPay name, marks, original text, generated imagery, design and code are protected by applicable rights. Third-party materials remain the property of their owners. Please obtain written permission before reproducing site assets.

Changes

We may update the site and this notice. A new “last updated” date will appear when the notice changes materially.

Contact

Questions about this notice go to contact@champ-pay.com.
