Partners

We did not set out to build every layer ourselves.

Payments is a partnership business. Licences, rails, settlement and distribution all belong to somebody, and the companies that pretend otherwise tend to be the ones that come unstuck.

Partner with us

The model

Five kinds of partner.

Each has a defined role and a defined boundary. We would rather be a good counterparty in five relationships than a mediocre owner of five capabilities.

Licensed institutions

Banks and licensed payment partners whose permissions the regulated parts of our products run on. The most important relationships we have.

Technology

Processing, card issuing, contactless kernels, identity and fraud tooling — the specialist components underneath the product.

Settlement & liquidity

Counterparties who move value reliably across currencies and corridors on defined timing, with terms in writing.

Distribution

Agent networks, merchant groups, franchises and associations who already hold the relationships we are building for.

Advisory & legal

Regulatory, licensing and corporate counsel in each jurisdiction we operate in. Specialist advice, taken early.

Something else
