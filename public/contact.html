Before you write

Protect the detail that makes the conversation valuable.

A good first note establishes fit. It should not contain information that requires an NDA, secure transfer or formal diligence route.

Will the contact form create an NDA?

No. If the next step requires confidential information, ask for an appropriate written NDA or NCNDA and wait until it is executed before disclosing that information.

What should I include about a payment use case?

The market, user type, payment method, operating problem and desired outcome. Do not send customer names, transaction files, card data, credentials or account information.

How are permissions and regulated roles discussed?

Through a scoped institutional conversation supported by the relevant documents. The public website does not publish the status of permissions, applications or counterparties.

Do you publish pricing?

Executable rates, fees, spreads and settlement terms depend on the specific use case and are handled privately. Market rates shown elsewhere on the site are indicative references only.

Can a supplier or specialist partner write?

Yes—if the note identifies the specific capability, market, evidence and ChampPay problem it addresses. The partners page explains the format.

How are job applications handled?

Use the candidate option, link to a CV or portfolio and explain what you are unusually good at. Applications arrive through the same monitored contact route.

No sensitive attachments in the first message. If a document is necessary, first agree the recipient, purpose and secure transfer route.
