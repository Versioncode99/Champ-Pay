The gap

Our position, stated plainly

What we hold, and what we do not

Most companies at our stage blur this line. We publish it, because everyone who matters is going to check anyway.

In place today

  • A registered Nigerian company and an in-market network of operator, banking and government relationships.
  • An active introducer mandate on an institutional liquidity chain, under contract.
  • A documented commercial framework covering exclusivity, non-circumvention, tail protection and fee terms.
  • Primary market research across nineteen African exchanges and the West African gaming corridor.

Not yet held, and not claimed

  • Card-scheme acquiring registration under Visa or Mastercard rules.
  • Gaming-authority licensing in any jurisdiction.
  • Bank facilitation, EMI or payment-institution permissions.
  • PCI DSS certification, and our own pay-in, payout or settlement rails.

Nothing on the left is aspirational. Nothing on the right is described anywhere on this site, in any document we send, or in any conversation we have, as something we already do. When that changes, this page changes with it.
