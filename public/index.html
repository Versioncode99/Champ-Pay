The product

Every Android phone is already a card terminal.

Nigeria runs on millions of POS terminals that somebody had to buy, carry, charge and replace. SoftPOS removes the box: a merchant taps a customer’s card or phone against the handset they already own, and the money moves.

Alongside it, static and dynamic QR for counters and market stalls, and proximity transfers that need no account number at all — designed for how trade actually happens in Lagos, not how it happens in a card-present shopping mall.

The market

Nigeria already went cashless. The infrastructure is still catching up.

The demand question was settled years ago. Nigerian electronic payments reached a record volume in 2025, and point-of-sale acceptance more than doubled year on year. What has not been settled is who serves the merchant at the bottom of that curve — the trader with one phone, no terminal, and a customer waiting.

That merchant is the whole thesis.
