# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Four audiences, all B2B, all evaluating whether ChampPay is credible enough to enter a conversation with. None of them are consumers; nobody signs up self-serve.

1. **Gaming & betting operators** — the merchants ChampPay intends to process for. Situation: running deposits and withdrawals in markets where settlement is slow, approval rates are poor, and payout failure costs them players. Job: find a payments partner who understands high-risk and can actually settle.
2. **Banks & sponsor partners** — needed for BIN sponsorship, correspondent access and facilitation. Situation: assessing counterparty risk before granting access. Job: decide whether this company is serious about compliance and governance, or a liability.
3. **Investors** — who would fund the licensing path. Job: understand the wedge, the market and why this team wins.
4. **Introducers & partners** — relationship-led deal originators. Job: decide whether ChampPay is credible enough to put in front of their own clients.

The common job across all four: *decide whether to take a meeting.* The site's success is a qualified inbound enquiry, not a conversion.

## Product Purpose

ChampPay is being built as a payments company for markets and verticals that established processors treat as too hard — with an initial focus on the gaming and betting vertical and on African payment corridors, Nigeria first.

Success for the site: a gaming operator, bank, investor or introducer reads it, concludes the company is serious and competent, and makes contact.

## Positioning

Africa-first payments built specifically for the high-risk and gaming vertical, by operators who already hold the market relationships on the ground rather than approaching the continent from outside.

The competitive set (Adyen, Checkout.com, Nuvei, Paysafe, Worldpay) is better capitalised and further ahead on licensing. ChampPay's differentiator is corridor and vertical focus plus in-market relationships, not scale, not price.

**Critical:** pricing/rate is explicitly *not* the position. Market evidence (Verto, operating $25B/yr in this corridor) is that businesses here now prioritise settlement speed over rate optimisation. Any rate-led argument is fighting yesterday's battle.

## Operating Context

Evaluation happens in a meeting or an inbox, often on mobile, often forwarded. The site is frequently the second thing seen after an email — it confirms or destroys a first impression. It will be opened alongside competitor sites during due diligence.

## Capabilities and Constraints

**Confirmed today:** an in-market Nigerian relationship network; a Phase 1 introducer arrangement on an institutional liquidity chain; a contract suite (introducer agreements, NCNDA); domain `champ-pay.com` on Cloudflare.

**Explicitly undecided / not yet held — must never be claimed:**
- Card-scheme acquiring registration (ISO/PayFac under Visa/Mastercard) — not held, not applied for.
- Gaming-authority licensing in any jurisdiction — not held.
- Bank facilitation / EMI / PI licensing — not held.
- PCI DSS certification — not held.
- VASP licensing — status unconfirmed.
- Own pay-in, payout or settlement rails — do not exist; the model is partner-or-build, undecided.

**Terminology:** "acquiring", "processing", "licensed", "regulated", "PCI compliant" are all reserved words on this site. They may describe the industry or the ambition. They may never describe ChampPay's present capability.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** ChampPay. Legal entity in footer: **Champ Pay Nigeria Limited**.
- **Slogan (binding):** "Pay Like a Champ" — on file since the May 2026 business plan.
- **Voice:** institutional and investor-register; conservative; counterparty-appropriate. This site may be read by a bank's risk team.
- **No logo or brand system exists.** Being created as part of this work.
- **Partners are not named publicly** — confirmed 2026-08-02. Liquidity and settlement relationships are referred to generically. No third party appears by name.

## Evidence on Hand

Real and usable: the domain; the contract suite; the market research (African crypto exchange intelligence report, May 2026); the strategic direction record; competitor design teardown (2026-08-02).

**Absences that must never be fabricated — this is the most important section of this file:**
- **No customers.** No client logos. No case studies. No testimonials.
- **No processing volume.** No transaction counts, no "$X processed", no uptime figures, no approval-rate statistics.
- **No team page content** beyond what is genuinely confirmed.
- **No certifications, licences, memberships or regulatory badges.**
- **No press coverage or awards.**

A new company earns credibility through clarity of thought and quality of execution. Every trust signal on this site must be real or must not exist. Inventing a statistic here is not a design shortcut — it is a false statement to a bank.

## Product Principles

1. **Never claim capability that does not exist.** Speak about direction, market understanding and intent in the present; speak about acquiring, processing and licensing only as the road ahead.
2. **Credibility comes from precision, not volume of claims.** Saying less, exactly, reads more institutional than saying more, vaguely.
3. **Design carries the trust that proof points normally would.** With no logos or numbers available, execution quality *is* the argument.
4. **Speed and understanding over price.** Never lead with rate.
5. **Written for someone who will check.** Assume a bank risk analyst reads every word.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Public-facing B2B site; target WCAG 2.2 AA. Content must remain readable and navigable with animation disabled (`prefers-reduced-motion`) and at 200% zoom. Read on mobile as often as desktop.
