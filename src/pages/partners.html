Privacy first

No public logo theatre.

A company’s name, mark and relationship are its information. ChampPay does not use a logo to imply a negotiation, endorsement or completed agreement.

Where a prospective partner needs non-public context, identity and authority are confirmed first, followed by appropriate written confidentiality and a scoped exchange.

Boundaries before brochures

What the first working session should settle.

The answers depend on the relationship. The questions should never be left implicit.

QuestionDecision requiredWhy it matters
Who owns the relationship?Origination, contracting, communications and non-circumvention.Prevents channel conflict and protects the party that created access.
Who owns risk?Approval, monitoring, limits, exceptions and reporting.Risk cannot be shared vaguely; control and accountability must align.
Who owns support?First line, escalation, disputes and incident communication.The merchant needs one clear route when a payment fails.
Who may use the data?Purpose, access, retention, security and deletion.Payment data should not become an undefined secondary asset.
How does value move?Accounts, instructions, reconciliation and failed-settlement handling.A diagram reveals gaps that prose can hide.
How does the relationship end?Notice, continuity, migration, records and surviving obligations.Exit design is cheaper before dependency exists.
